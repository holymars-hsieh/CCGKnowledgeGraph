* **What is it?** {{One sentence definition}}
* **Context:** Where does this system exist? (e.g., Biological Brain, Market, Social Network).
* **Goal:** What is this system trying to maximize or minimize? (e.g., Free Energy, Profit, Stability).

## 2. Isomorphism (The Mapping)

* **Metaphor:** "This behaves like a [Thermodynamic Engine / Ecological Niche / Market Ticker]."
* **Input/Output:** What goes in? What comes out?

## 3. System Architecture

### 3.1 Substrate

* **Agents/Nodes:** Who are the actors?
* **Connections:** How are they linked? (Topology).
* **Constraints:** What is physically impossible here?

### 3.2 Logic

* **Mechanism:** How does information flow? (Step-by-Step).
* **Incentives:** What drives the agents? (The Loss Function).

## 4. Dynamics

* **Feedback Loops:**

* **Phase Transitions:** When does the system break or change state? (e.g., Water -> Steam).
* **Entropy:** Does the system tend towards disorder (Heat Death) or structure (Life)?
